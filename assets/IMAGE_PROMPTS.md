# Image generation prompts

These project images were created with the built-in imagegen tool in ads-marketing mode and edited with its precise-object-edit mode. The final source plates are in this directory.

## `seal-press.jpg`

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: cinematic video scene plate, 16:9 landscape. Primary request: extreme close-up of one adult hand pressing an old dark brass wax seal stamp vertically onto deep burgundy wax on an ivory envelope lying on a dark charcoal walnut trading desk. The stamp is in contact with the wax, with pressure visible in the fingertips. A large out-of-focus monitor in the upper background shows a faint glowing mint-green candlestick chart. Premium photographic realism, macro lens, shallow depth of field, tactile paper and warm brass, dark navy shadows, charcoal surfaces, restrained mint-green accents. Composition leaves the seal and hand in center-right and visible envelope in lower half. No faces or bodies beyond the hand. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

## `seal-hover.jpg`

Edit of `seal-press.jpg`:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. This image is an edit target. Keep the exact same camera angle, hand, stamp, envelope, desk, monitor, lighting, and composition. Change only the stamp and hand position: lift the stamp approximately 8 cm vertically above the soft burgundy wax, so its underside is visibly hovering above the envelope before pressing. Preserve the exact style and every other object. No new text, no logos, no watermark.

## `ledger-closing.jpg`

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: cinematic video scene plate, 16:9 landscape. Primary request: a thick, old ledger on a dark charcoal desk, its heavy navy leather front cover partway down in the act of closing, pages visible along the right edge and underneath. Three-quarter overhead close-up. The cover is blank and unmarked with ample clean central area for text to be composited later. Dark navy and charcoal cinematic lighting, thin mint-green reflected edge light, realistic leather grain and cream page edges, strong shadows, premium product commercial photography, shallow depth of field. No hands, no people, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

## `ledger-closed.jpg`

Edit of `ledger-closing.jpg`:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. This image is an edit target. Keep the exact same desk, camera angle, lighting, book position and overall composition. Change only the ledger front cover from slightly ajar to completely closed flat against the thick pages, hiding the open wedge of pages at the top right while leaving the book's thick page block visible at the lower and right edges. Keep the cover clean blank navy leather for text compositing. No text, no logos, no watermark.
